## Hero artwork

Built-in image-generation tool; concept illustration, not photographic evidence or numerical output. The generated master is encoded as two WebP sizes without changing the scene:

- `assets/offshore-hero.webp` — 1774 × 887.
- `assets/offshore-hero-mobile.webp` — 960 × 480.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide hero image for a professional offshore geotechnical research website, in a minimalist premium product-presentation aesthetic. Create a beautifully lit photorealistic 3D studio concept render of an offshore wind turbine on a single cylindrical steel monopile, with an elegant cutaway revealing the embedded monopile beneath the seabed. One main white three-bladed turbine with yellow transition piece, two smaller turbines in the distant blue-grey sea to establish offshore wind context. Show a calm blue water slice over a pale sand cross-section; the hollow cylindrical monopile should visibly continue straight down into the soil. Physical proportions are illustrative and not an engineering drawing. Wide landscape composition about 2:1, strong subject silhouette, generous pale silver-white negative space above and around, quiet horizon fading to white, precise machined surfaces, subtle sand texture, soft shadows and luminous natural light. Restrained palette of white, silver, deep ocean blue and a small safety-yellow accent. Elegant high-end editorial product render, not cartoon, not infographic. Entire turbine rotor and below-seabed foundation visible, no cropped blades. No text, numbers, labels, arrows, data overlays, logos, brands, people, watermarks, or decorative UI.
